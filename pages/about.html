Our story

Why we dig into soil

Soil is the foundation of almost all life on land — yet it's one of the most overlooked and misunderstood parts of our natural world. We're here to change that.

Our mission

Improving soil literacy for everyone

Soil Edu exists to improve soil literacy across all ages and backgrounds. We do this through engaging digital content, animation, books, comics, learning resources, outreach programmes, and accessible research communication.

We believe that when people understand soil — really understand it — they become passionate advocates for protecting it. And healthy soil means a healthier planet for all of us.

Core message: Discover the hidden world beneath our feet.
Tagline: Learning from the Ground Up.
🌍

Our Vision

A world where every child grows up understanding, valuing, and protecting the soil beneath their feet — because they know the remarkable hidden world it contains.

Our values

What drives everything we do

🔬

Science-Led

Everything we produce is grounded in real, peer-reviewed soil science. We work with researchers to ensure accuracy without sacrificing accessibility.

🎨

Creatively Courageous

We believe learning happens when you're captivated. We invest in beautiful design, compelling stories, and imaginative characters that make soil science irresistible.

🌍

Globally Relevant

Soil health is a global crisis and a global opportunity. Our content is designed to resonate across cultures and be adaptable to different educational contexts worldwide.

🏫

Educator-Centred

Teachers are our most important partners. We design every resource to be genuinely useful in real classrooms, with minimal preparation time required.

🆓

Open Access

Core learning resources will always be free. We believe soil literacy shouldn't depend on budget — every classroom deserves access to quality soil education.

🤝

Community-Powered

We grow through collaboration — with educators, researchers, children, parents, and local organisations who share our passion for the underground world.

Our journey

How Soil Edu grew

2021

The idea takes root

Founded after recognising a gap in soil science education resources for children and schools.

2022

First content produced

Early YouTube videos and downloadable worksheets launched to an enthusiastic audience of educators.

2023

Soil Rangers concept developed

Characters and story world for the animated series created through workshops with primary school children.

2024

Research partnerships formed

Formal collaborations with soil science researchers established to power the Research to Reality programme.

2025–26

Platform launch

Full Soil Edu digital platform launched — the central home for all content, resources, and community.

Why soil literacy matters

The urgency beneath our feet

Soil is under extraordinary pressure. Intensive agriculture, deforestation, pollution, and urbanisation are degrading soils at an alarming rate. Yet most people know almost nothing about soil.

This matters because:

95% of our food is produced directly or indirectly from soil.
Soil stores 3× more carbon than all vegetation and the atmosphere combined.
Over a third of the world's soil is already degraded due to human activity.

By building soil literacy — especially in young people — we create advocates who will make better decisions about land use, food choices, and environmental policy throughout their lives.
