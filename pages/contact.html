Frequently asked

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Drop us a message above.

Are the learning resources really free? ▼
Yes — all core learning resources on the Learning Hub are free to download and use. We believe every school and family should have access to quality soil education regardless of budget. Some premium resource packs may require registration.
How do I book a school visit? ▼
Use the contact form above and select "School / Educator" as your enquiry type. Tell us your school, year group, approximate dates, and number of pupils. We'll get back to you within 2 working days to confirm availability and discuss the format.
Can I use Soil Edu content in my curriculum? ▼
Absolutely. All resources are designed for classroom use and are free for non-commercial educational purposes. For any commercial or broadcast use of Soil Rangers content or illustrations, please contact us to discuss licensing.
I'm a researcher — can I write for the Research to Reality section? ▼
We'd love to work with you. Use the contact form and select "Partnership" — tell us about your research and we'll be in touch to discuss how we might collaborate. We work with scientists to co-create accessible articles and videos.
When will Soil Rangers books be available? ▼
The first Soil Rangers picture book (Wriggles and the Underground World) is available to pre-order now and will ship in late 2026. Sign up to our newsletter to be notified the moment it's available — and to hear about future titles.
Do you offer school subscriptions or memberships? ▼
A school membership programme is coming in Phase 2 of the platform, planned for 2027. It will include access to an expanded resource library, premium video content, and priority booking for outreach visits. Sign up to the newsletter to stay informed.
