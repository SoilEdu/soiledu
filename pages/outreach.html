How we bring soil science to life

From classroom visits to multi-day residencies, our outreach programmes are designed for real impact — leaving children with a lasting connection to the soil world.

Primary & Secondary

School Visits

Our team brings soil science directly to your classroom — with hands-on activities, microscopes, live soil samples, and a whole lot of enthusiasm.

  • Half-day or full-day session formats
  • Live soil microscopy — watch organisms move
  • Soil texture and composition experiments
  • Curriculum-linked to science standards
  • Take-home resource pack for every child
  • Post-visit worksheet and teacher resources
Available for: Early Years, KS1, KS2, KS3
Group size: Up to 32 children per session

Outdoor Learning

Soil & Garden Programme

A term-long outdoor learning programme that transforms school gardens and community plots into living soil science labs.

  • 6-week or 12-week programme structure
  • Set up a school composting system
  • Long-term soil health monitoring
  • Seasonal planting linked to soil topics
  • Pupil-led data collection and recording
  • End-of-programme celebration event
Best for: Schools with garden space
Duration: 6 or 12 weeks

How it works

Booking a school visit

Getting Soil Edu into your school is straightforward. Here's how the process works from initial enquiry to visit day.

1

Send an enquiry

Fill in our contact form with your school, year group, preferred dates, and any specific topics you'd like us to cover.

2

We'll get back to you

Within 3 working days we'll confirm availability, discuss the session format, and answer any questions.

3

Confirm and prepare

We'll send a preparation guide so your class is ready to dig in. No prior soil knowledge required — we bring everything.

4

Visit day!

We arrive with all equipment, materials, and enthusiasm. You just need a classroom — or better yet, some outdoor space.
