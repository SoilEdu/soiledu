About the series

Underground heroes for the next generation

Soil Rangers follows a team of miniature underground characters who live in the soil and work together to protect it from threats — pollution, erosion, compaction, and drought. Each episode is built around real soil science concepts, making it a perfect companion for primary science curricula.

The series is designed for children aged 4–12, with stories entertaining enough to captivate young viewers and science accurate enough to satisfy their teachers.

Meet the team

The Soil Rangers

Six unique underground characters, each representing a different part of the soil ecosystem.

🪱

Wriggles

Earthworm · Team Leader

The brave leader of the Soil Rangers. Wriggles tunnels through soil creating pathways for water and roots, and rallies the team when danger threatens the underground world.

Main character
🍄

Mykie

Mycorrhizal Fungus · Communicator

The quiet but wise network-builder. Mykie connects plant roots across the underground, passing nutrients and messages through an invisible web of fungal threads.

Main character
🐛

Cater

Caterpillar Larva · Engineer

The team's practical problem-solver. Cater knows everything about soil structure and porosity, and can always find a way to fix a compacted section or restore a flood channel.

Main character
🦠

Micro

Soil Bacterium · Scientist

Tiny but brilliant. Micro is always busy breaking down organic matter and cycling nutrients — and loves explaining the science behind everything the Rangers encounter.

Supporting character
🐜

Anty

Soil Ant · Organiser

The hard-working team organiser. Anty coordinates the Rangers' missions, keeps track of the soil's health, and is always the first to spot trouble from the surface above.

Supporting character
🌱

Sprout

Seedling Root · Newcomer

The newest and youngest Ranger — a seedling that has just pushed its roots into the soil world. Sprout asks all the questions that children want to ask, making it easy for viewers to learn alongside them.

Supporting character
